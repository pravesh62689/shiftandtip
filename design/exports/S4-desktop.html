gavel Statutory Preemption & Dual-Job Principles

Under federal law (29 U.S.C. § 203(m)), state statutes providing greater employee protection supersede federal limits. In states with $0.00 max allowable tip credit, employers MUST pay the full statutory baseline wage in direct cash prior to any employee tip earnings. Furthermore, under DOL 80/20 guidance, non-tipped prep work exceeding 20% of an employee's scheduled workweek cannot be subjected to a tip credit.
