## Brand & Style
This design system embodies a calm, authoritative US payroll-utility aesthetic with an understated editorial dignity. Engineered for hourly workers, shift managers, and payroll accountants, it prioritizes clarity, trust, and absolute zero hype over visual novelty. The visual tone evokes archival bookkeeping, federal tax forms, and classic printed paper ledgers—translating their legibility, deliberate pacing, and structural discipline into a functional digital surface.

The design movement is a grounded hybrid of **Editorial Functionalism** and **Tactile Ledger Realism**:
- **Utilitarian Discipline:** Visual weight is established through typographic hierarchy, subtle horizontal ruling, and micro-bordered surfaces rather than soft elevations, gradients, or ambient blur effects.
- **Printed Matter Metaphor:** Interfaces mimic physical slips, perforated slips, tabular receipts, and double-entry paper manifests through dashed dividers, tactile container groupings, and monospaced financial outputs.
- **Tone & Demeanor:** Sober, dependable, and objective. Financial data and legal compliance notices are communicated transparently without celebratory micro-animations, neon gamification, or emotional hyperbole.

## Colors
The palette is calibrated strictly for light mode, drawing directly from physical archival paper stock, forest-green ledger inks, and oxidized receipt markings. High-contrast, clean lines handle all content structuring.

### Functional Palette
- **Paper Background (`#F7F6F1`):** A warm, low-glare canvas base simulating heavy-weight uncoated paper stock.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white used exclusively on contained surfaces, interactive cards, and primary input zones to maintain contrast over the paper background.
- **Ad & Utility Surface (`#F1EFE8`):** Subdued sand tone for ancillary sidebars, promotional utility blocks, or background highlights.
- **Ink Primary (`#16201C`):** High-density deep slate ink for all primary headers, figures, and high-emphasis body text.
- **Ink Secondary (`#3D4A44`):** Medium-density ink for labels, subtitles, table keys, and instructional prose.
- **Muted Ink (`#5E6B65`):** Reserved for de-emphasized metadata, timestamps, helper descriptions, and input placeholders.
- **Line (`#E3E0D6`):** The primary boundary token for all card borders, grid gutters, and field inputs.
- **Line-2 (`#CFCABB`):** High-contrast structural line for table header divides, pinned footers, and active element perimeters.
- **Ledger Green (`#0E6B4C` / `#0A5039` hover):** Primary action token signifying verified calculation, balance approvals, and primary CTA triggers. Accompanied by **Ledger-50 (`#E7F1EC`)** for badges, active states, and success notifications.
- **Receipt Accent (`#A85400`):** An amber-burnt ochre for regulatory calls-to-action, hourly rate variations, and warnings. Accompanied by **Receipt-50 (`#FBF1E3`)** for callout background fills.
- **Danger (`#B42318`):** Strict red for compliance infractions, payroll calculation deficits, and input errors.

No purple, violet, indigo, neon hues, or multi-stop gradients are permitted anywhere across the interface.

## Typography
Typographic discipline is non-negotiable. Only three font families exist within this system: **Source Serif 4**, **Public Sans**, and **IBM Plex Mono**. Under no circumstances may Inter, Poppins, Roboto, or Montserrat be substituted.

- **Source Serif 4 (SemiBold 600):** Anchors section titles, calculating engine headers, and major editorial points. Gives numerical tools an established, institutional voice.
- **Public Sans (Regular 400 & SemiBold 600):** Drives all standard application interfaces, instructions, form field labels, legal disclaimers, and running body text. Designed for uncompromising government and institutional legibility.
- **IBM Plex Mono (Medium 500 Tabular):** Dedicated strictly to currency amounts, tip distribution percentages, shift durations, clock timestamps, and tax deductions. All financial outputs, calculation results, and table columns must use this family with open tabular numbers enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres strictly to an 8-point base rhythm, anchored around a 4px sub-grid for tight numerical alignment. The top-level content conforms to a centered 12-column fixed container capping at 1200px.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 1200px max-width container, 24px gutters (`gutter-desktop`), 32px page margins (`margin-desktop`). Split-view calculation interfaces align inputs to the left (7 columns) and live receipt outputs to the right (5 columns).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, full-width container, 24px gutters (`gutter-tablet`), 24px canvas margins (`margin-tablet`). Calculation manifests stack under form inputs.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns, fluid width, 16px gutters (`gutter`), 16px screen margins (`margin`). Dense data tables enable lateral horizontal scrolls with fixed first-column anchors.
- **Horizontal Ledger Ruling:** Sections within cards and structural lists rely on explicit `1px solid #E3E0D6` borders rather than wide margins, keeping information density compact and scan rates rapid.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is planar, tactile, and physical—achieved through crisp linework and stacked paper layers rather than atmospheric shadows or synthetic glow.

- **Structural Linework Over Shadow:** 1px borders using `line` (`#E3E0D6`) or `line-2` (`#CFCABB`) establish all structural perimeters.
- **Shadow Limitation:** True shadow is nearly prohibited across the system. A singular ambient drop shadow token is permitted:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(22, 32, 28, 0.06);`
  This shadow is reserved exclusively for primary floating containers (e.g., active dropdown menus, sticky calculate bars on mobile, and raised receipt cards). 
- **Surface Layering:** Depth is conveyed by contrasting `#F7F6F1` (the desk paper base) against `#FFFFFF` (the physical card/slip on top). Active selections and inputs utilize `#E7F1EC` (ledger-50) or white surfaces with a high-contrast `#0E6B4C` or `#16201C` hairline frame.
- **No Diffusion:** No heavy multi-tier blur shadows, no zero-border floating components, and no frosted glass backdrop blurs are permitted.

## Shapes
The shape language combines utilitarian rectangularity with measured, functional corner softening:

- **6px Radius (`border-radius: 6px`):** Applied uniformly to inputs, textareas, standard utility buttons, table filter toggles, and dropdown selectors. This provides a clean, precise, non-childlike finish.
- **10px Radius (`border-radius: 10px`):** Applied to top-level content cards, perforated receipt wrappers, compliance banners, and application containers.
- **999px Radius (Full Pill):** Exclusively designated for status chips, compliance tags, and small verification badges. Primary buttons and input fields must never use pill rounding.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Ledger):** Background `#0E6B4C`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius 6px, font `Public Sans 15px/600`, padding `10px 18px`. Hover: `#0A5039`. Active: translate Y `1px`.
- **Secondary Action (Paper Outline):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CFCABB`, text `#16201C`, font `Public Sans 15px/600`. Hover: `#F7F6F1`.
- **Destructive Action:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #B42318`, text `#B42318`. Hover: `#B42318` text with `#FBF1E3` background.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Numeric & Form Fields:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E3E0D6`, border-radius 6px, padding `10px 12px`, text `Public Sans 15px/400`. Focus state: border `1px solid #0E6B4C`, outline `2px solid #E7F1EC`.
- **Currency/Numeric Inputs:** Prefix/suffix currency indicators ($ or %) rendered in `IBM Plex Mono 15px/500` with text color `#5E6B65`. All typed numeric inputs render in `IBM Plex Mono 500`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio), border `1.5px solid #CFCABB`, border-radius 3px (checkbox) or 50% (radio). Selected: background `#0E6B4C`, border-color `#0E6B4C`, white check/dot icon.

### Perforated Receipt Result Cards
- Dedicated presentation container for net pay, tip pool distributions, and overtime reconciliations.
- Built on a `#FFFFFF` card with `10px` radius and `1px solid #E3E0D6` border.
- Features a **dashed horizontal perforation divider**: `border-top: 1.5px dashed #CFCABB` separating line-item hours from net figures.
- Top section contains tabular breakdowns (`Public Sans 15px` labels with `IBM Plex Mono 15px` values).
- Bottom summary section contains large, emphasized totals formatted in `IBM Plex Mono 24px/500` under `#16201C`.

### Ledger Data Tables
- Horizontal rule-only architecture (`1px solid #E3E0D6`). No vertical column separator lines.
- Column headers: `Public Sans 13px/600`, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.05em`, color `#5E6B65`, background `#F7F6F1`.
- Row cells: Height 44px, padding `0 12px`, borders along bottom only. Numeric columns right-aligned and formatted in `IBM Plex Mono 15px/500`. Alternating rows remain uncolored; hover adds `#F7F6F1` tint.

### Chips & Badges
- Strict pill design (`border-radius: 999px`), padding `3px 10px`, font `Public Sans 13px/600`.
- **Standard Chip:** Border `1px solid #E3E0D6`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#3D4A44`.
- **Verified Compliance Badge:** Background `#E7F1EC`, border `1px solid #0E6B4C`, text `#0A5039`, prepended with a monochrome geometric checkmark icon.

### Editorial Regulatory Callouts
- Used for FLSA overtime warnings, tip-credit wage laws, and deduction mandates.
- Background `#FBF1E3` (receipt-50), border `1px solid #E3E0D6`, border-left `3px solid #A85400` (receipt accent), border-radius `0 6px 6px 0`.
- Body text set in `Public Sans 15px/22px`, heading in `Public Sans 15px/600` colored `#A85400`.
