## Detailed Profiles

### 1. Restaurant Payroll
- **Gusto**: The premier payroll solution for independent restaurants, cafes, and hospitality venues. Excellent self-service onboarding and automated tip reporting. Top converting payroll offer on PartnerStack/Impact.
- **ADP RUN**: Heavy enterprise brand equity. Highest single-transaction bounties ($250–$500), suited for multi-unit restaurant managers calculating complex multi-state overtime.
- **Paychex**: Strong choice for mid-sized hospitality groups seeking dedicated payroll reps and 401(k) integration.

### 2. Scheduling & Tip Distribution Software
- **7shifts**: Built specifically for restaurants. Direct software solution for the exact pain point addressed by our Tip Pool and Tip-Out tools (automated tip pooling calculations and POS sync). 90-day cookie length provides long attribution window.
- **Homebase**: Dominates small hourly shift tracking, free scheduling tiers, and mobile shift clocking. High conversion rate among hourly restaurant workers and shift managers.
- **Toast POS & Payroll**: High-ticket bounties ($500–$1,000) for complete restaurant management systems.
- **Sling**: Lightweight scheduling and time clock app popular with quick-service restaurants and cafes.

### 3. Tax Filing Software (Jan–Apr Priority)
- **TurboTax (Intuit)**: Over 70% consumer mindshare in DIY US tax prep. Extremely strong conversion on our Take-Home and Section 199A/Tip Deduction estimators during tax season.
- **TaxAct / H&R Block / FreeTaxUSA**: High-converting alternatives for budget-conscious tipped hourly workers looking to maximize refunds and claim tip tax credits.
