How this was calculated

  1. Tip-outs come off the top: 3% of $8,000 net sales = $240.00 to the kitchen.
  2. The remaining $1,000.00 is shared by hours: 30.5 hours in total.
  3. Each person gets their hours ÷ 30.5 of the pool, rounded so every cent is allocated.
