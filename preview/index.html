1. Tip Pool Calculator data-tool="tip-pool"

Distributes shift tip pools by hours worked, points, or percentage, with top-slice kitchen/support tip-outs.

2. Tip-Out Calculator data-tool="tip-out"

Calculates server tip-outs to bar, bussers, and runners from net sales or total tips.

3. Overtime & Double Time Calculator data-tool="overtime"

Models weekly and daily overtime + 7th consecutive day rules (CA, CO, AK, NV, Federal).

4. Shift Differential & Blended Rate Calculator data-tool="shift-differential"

Calculates weighted average regular rate and overtime premiums across mixed base and premium shifts.

5. Tipped Overtime Calculator data-tool="tipped-overtime"

Calculates correct statutory tipped overtime cash wage based on full minimum wage minus allowable tip credit.

6. Take-Home Pay with Tips Calculator data-tool="take-home"

Estimates pay-period take-home after Social Security, Medicare, Federal income tax, and state tax exemptions.

7. No-Tax-on-Tips & Overtime Deduction Estimator data-tool="tips-ot-deduction"

Models federal above-the-line deduction caps ($25k tips, $12.5k/$25k OT) with MAGI phase-out reductions.
