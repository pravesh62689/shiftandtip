## How this was calculated

Wages and tips are added together. Social Security and Medicare are applied to the total, Social Security up to the annual wage base. Federal and state income tax use the rates you enter.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving cash tips out. They are taxable income.
- Treating the result as an exact paycheck. Withholding depends on your W-4.
- Entering a state rate for a state with no wage income tax.
