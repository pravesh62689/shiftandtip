## How this was calculated

Daily rules apply first in states that have them, then any regular hours beyond the weekly threshold convert to overtime, latest days first, so no hour is paid twice.

## Common mistakes

- Starting the week on the wrong day. Use your employer's workweek.
- Assuming every state has daily overtime. Most follow the federal weekly rule.
- Forgetting that bonuses and shift differentials change the regular rate.
