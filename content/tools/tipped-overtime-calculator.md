## How this was calculated

Overtime is one and a half times the full minimum wage. The tip credit stays the same, so the cash wage for overtime hours is the overtime rate minus the credit.

## Common mistakes

- Paying one and a half times the low cash wage.
- Using the federal numbers in a state that sets different ones.
- Forgetting that tips plus cash wages must still reach the full minimum wage.
