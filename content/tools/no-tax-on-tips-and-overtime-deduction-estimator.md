## How this was calculated

Each deduction is capped by filing status, then reduced when modified adjusted gross income passes the phase-out start. Only the premium half of time-and-a-half counts toward the overtime deduction.

## Common mistakes

- Counting the full overtime pay instead of just the premium.
- Assuming state daily overtime always qualifies.
- Filing separately, which gets no deduction under these rules.
