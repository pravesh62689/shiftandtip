## How this was calculated

Each tip-out is a percentage of either your net sales or your tips, depending on your restaurant's policy. The calculator adds the tip-outs together and subtracts them from your tips.

## Common mistakes

- Using the wrong base. Some restaurants tip out on sales, others on tips.
- Forgetting that the percentages in the form are examples, not an industry standard.
- Tipping out on gross sales when your policy uses net sales after discounts.
