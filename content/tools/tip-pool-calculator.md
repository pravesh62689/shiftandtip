## How this was calculated

The calculator takes each tip-out off the top first, then divides what is left in proportion to each person's hours, points, or percentage. Shares are rounded with the largest-remainder method, so the payouts always add up to the exact amount in the pool.

## Common mistakes

- Putting a manager or supervisor in the pool. Under federal law they cannot keep tips from it.
- Running tip-outs on net sales when the house rule is a share of tips, or the other way around.
- Rounding each share separately, which leaves a few cents unallocated.
