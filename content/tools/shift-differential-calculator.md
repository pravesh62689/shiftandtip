## How this was calculated

The regular rate is all straight-time pay for the week, plus any nondiscretionary bonus, divided by all hours worked. Each overtime hour then earns half of that rate on top of the straight time already paid.

## Common mistakes

- Paying overtime on the base rate only.
- Leaving out nondiscretionary bonuses, which belong in the regular rate.
- Counting discretionary bonuses, which do not.
