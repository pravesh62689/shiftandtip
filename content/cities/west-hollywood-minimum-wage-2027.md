## West Hollywood Municipal Wage Standard

Non-exempt employees working within the geographic boundaries of West Hollywood are entitled to a local minimum wage of **$19.08 per hour**, established under West Hollywood Municipal Code 5.130. Because West Hollywood's municipal wage exceeds both the federal FLSA standard ($7.25/hr) and the California state minimum wage ($16.90/hr), employers operating within city limits must pay the higher local ordinance rate.

Workers are generally covered by the municipal ordinance if they perform two or more hours of work per week within the geographic limits of West Hollywood.

## Key Local Wage Standards

- **Municipal Minimum Wage Floor**: $19.08 per hour, effective 2025-07-01.
- **Premium Over State Baseline**: +$2.18/hr higher than the general California statutory rate.
- **Overtime Rate (1.5x)**: $28.62 per hour for hours exceeding standard thresholds.
- **Statutory Authority**: Codified under West Hollywood Municipal Code 5.130.

## Worked Example 1: Standard 40-Hour Workweek in West Hollywood

Sam works as a retail sales associate in West Hollywood earning the local statutory minimum wage of $19.08 per hour. Over a single standard workweek, Sam completes five 8-hour shifts totaling 40 hours.

Computation:
- Regular Hours: 40 hours
- Hourly Rate: $19.08/hr
- **Gross Weekly Compensation**: 40 × $19.08 = **$763.20**

Comparing against state baseline pay: at the base California rate of $16.90/hr, Sam would have earned $676.00. The West Hollywood local ordinance provides an extra $87.20 per week.

## Worked Example 2: 45-Hour Shift with Overtime

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the West Hollywood rate apply to remote workers?
The municipal rate applies to hours physically performed within the jurisdictional limits of West Hollywood. Remote workers living and performing tasks inside city boundaries are typically protected by the ordinance.

### Can employers pay a lower training wage?
Under West Hollywood Municipal Code 5.130, exemptions are strictly limited. Most commercial employers must pay the full $19.08/hr wage floor from day one of employment.

### What should workers do if paid below $19.08/hr?
Employees subjected to wage violations can file a confidential complaint with the local labor standards enforcement division or claim back pay plus liquidated damages through the state labor commission.

### How often does West Hollywood adjust its minimum wage?
Local minimum wage rates are adjusted annually, typically indexed to the Consumer Price Index (CPI) to protect purchasing power against inflation.
