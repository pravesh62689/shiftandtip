## Portland Municipal Wage Standard

Non-exempt employees working within the geographic boundaries of Portland are entitled to a local minimum wage of **$15.95 per hour**, established under Oregon Metro Urban Growth Boundary Rate. Because Portland's municipal wage exceeds both the federal FLSA standard ($7.25/hr) and the Oregon state minimum wage ($15.55/hr), employers operating within city limits must pay the higher local ordinance rate.

Workers are generally covered by the municipal ordinance if they perform two or more hours of work per week within the geographic limits of Portland.

## Key Local Wage Standards

- **Municipal Minimum Wage Floor**: $15.95 per hour, effective 2025-07-01.
- **Premium Over State Baseline**: +$0.40/hr higher than the general Oregon statutory rate.
- **Overtime Rate (1.5x)**: $23.92 per hour for hours exceeding standard thresholds.
- **Statutory Authority**: Codified under Oregon Metro Urban Growth Boundary Rate.

## Worked Example 1: Standard 40-Hour Workweek in Portland

Sam works as a retail sales associate in Portland earning the local statutory minimum wage of $15.95 per hour. Over a single standard workweek, Sam completes five 8-hour shifts totaling 40 hours.

Computation:
- Regular Hours: 40 hours
- Hourly Rate: $15.95/hr
- **Gross Weekly Compensation**: 40 × $15.95 = **$638.00**

Comparing against state baseline pay: at the base Oregon rate of $15.55/hr, Sam would have earned $622.00. The Portland local ordinance provides an extra $16.00 per week.

## Worked Example 2: 45-Hour Shift with Overtime

Taylor works in hospitality in Portland logging 45 total hours during a festival week. Overtime is paid at 1.5 times the local statutory minimum wage ($23.92/hr) for all hours worked past 40.

Audit of compensation:
- Straight-time pay: 40 hours × $15.95 = $638.00
- Overtime premium pay: 5 hours × $23.92 = $119.62
- **Total Gross Pay**: **$757.63**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the Portland rate apply to remote workers?
The municipal rate applies to hours physically performed within the jurisdictional limits of Portland. Remote workers living and performing tasks inside city boundaries are typically protected by the ordinance.

### Can employers pay a lower training wage?
Under Oregon Metro Urban Growth Boundary Rate, exemptions are strictly limited. Most commercial employers must pay the full $15.95/hr wage floor from day one of employment.

### What should workers do if paid below $15.95/hr?
Employees subjected to wage violations can file a confidential complaint with the local labor standards enforcement division or claim back pay plus liquidated damages through the state labor commission.

### How often does Portland adjust its minimum wage?
Local minimum wage rates are adjusted annually, typically indexed to the Consumer Price Index (CPI) to protect purchasing power against inflation.
